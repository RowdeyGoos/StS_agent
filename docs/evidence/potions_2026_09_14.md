## Implemented boundaries

- Immutable definitions dispatch through small potion modules and the existing
  combat resolution queue. Use consumes the owned bottle before effects; card
  choices and nested autoplay/exhaust/draw work finish before Reptile Trinket and
  Unceasing Top. Snapshot continuations contain plain IDs, targets and cursors.
- All manual definitions execute. Selection, card generation, delayed powers,
  damage/status/block modifiers, local card costs and automatic revival use the
  existing gameplay paths. Combat inventories synchronize with run ownership;
  inventory and generation pools are installed before opening combat effects.
- Fairy in a Bottle consumes the first available Fairy before Lizard Tail and
  restores at least 1 HP, including lethal event damage. Blood Potion, Fruit Juice
  and Entropic Brew also work between room/event decisions. Event resource replay
  records their ordered inventory changes and rejects reused bottle identities.
- Generated runs, ordinary merchant stock and supported potion-granting events
  use all 48 ordinary potions. Factory draws use 65/25/10 percent common/uncommon/
  rare, then uniform selection. Native multi-potion factory calls avoid duplicate
  definitions. Alchemize excludes Fairy, Fruit Juice and Regen; Entropic Brew uses
  the unrestricted factory even during combat and fills its newly freed slot.
- Private snapshots advance to combat **v11** and run **v21**. Owned card state
  includes combat-free costs and temporary absolute Snecko overrides; cost RNG
  is explicitly saved. Legacy RL vocabulary and bridge contracts remain unchanged.

## Review findings

An independent semantic review checked pinned use/factory ordering, pending
choices, automatic revival and persistence. Corrected cases include Fairy before
Lizard Tail, Liquid Memories being free for this turn, Snecko replacing earlier
local discounts, Touch excluding X-cost/locally free cards, and final Tangled
cost clamping. Potion completion must remain behind all child work in the queue;
event history cannot resurrect a consumed bottle. Foul Potion's ordinary merchant
use grants 100 gold without disabling subsequent trading.

Beetle Juice's Shrink decreases each enemy turn. Rend counts Demise and counts
actual negative Strength once; it excludes the temporary Mangle/Shackles wrapper
powers. A separate pinned check confirmed that Weak, Vulnerable and negative
Strength yield 45 Rend damage in the existing regression, correcting its older
37-damage expectation. The bounded final reviews reported no remaining blockers.

## Remaining fidelity limits

Authored small-pool fixtures retain their explicit sampling and merchant layout;
the generated merchant still has its authored two potion slots. Native seed/draw
parity, unlock progression and native full merchant construction remain separate
tasks. Foul and Glowwater have executable effects, but their granting events and
Fake Merchant are not implemented. Other-character potion mechanics, multiplayer,
later acts, ascensions and exhaustive interaction/differential evidence remain
outside this solo A0 Act 1 scope. See the [current guide](../HEADLESS_ENGINE.md#potions).
